# Raaste — Product Context

## What It Is

A personal travel guide app built specifically for Indian domestic travel. Not a booking app. Not a delivery app. A guide.

## The Core Problem

Indian travellers show up places clueless: Uber doesn't work, chikki shops are tourist traps, the famous waterfall is overcrowded, the better one is 3km away. Raaste is the local friend who tells you these things in 2 minutes.

## How It Works

1. User enters destination and trip length.
2. One-time profile: travel style, food preferences, companions, budget, interests.
3. App builds personalised guidance across three phases.

## The Three Phases

### 1. Before the trip — The Researcher

- Opinionated destination briefing
- Real transport info and actual costs
- Day-by-day itinerary based on preferences
- Advance booking reminders
- Packing list for destination + season
- Local customs and safety info
- Price disclaimer on every cost

### 2. During the trip — The Companion

- Ask anything about current location
- Dietary-aware restaurant suggestions
- Local cab/transport guidance
- Offline core content
- Day-by-day checklist

### 3. After the trip — The Debrief

- 3-question review: great, disappointing, outdated
- Feedback improves knowledge base
- Community data becomes the moat

## Why It Beats Zomato / Google Maps / YouTube

- Judgment, not just ratings
- Personalised to traveller profile
- Transport + crowd + connectivity + timing in one place
- Built for India-specific realities

## India-Specific Focus

- Uber doesn't work everywhere
- Local cab rates are negotiable
- Veg / Jain / Halal requirements are serious
- Budget ranges in ₹
- Religious site dress codes and timings
- Monsoon accessibility changes
- Family vs friends vs solo trips are different experiences

## Dietary & Preference Layer

Built-in from day one: veg, non-veg, Jain, halal, vegan. Restaurant suggestions filtered automatically. Gets richer with community reviews.

## Monetisation

- No subscription
- One-time trip pass per destination (~₹29–₹49)
- Free version: top things to do, basic transport, general tips
- Paid version: full itinerary, companion, offline, checklists
- Contextual ads during active trip (cab, restaurant, experiences)
- Verified partner fee for genuinely good local operators

## Why Now

Indian domestic tourism is at an all-time high. Post-COVID young urban travellers are digitally native, travel on weekends, and need real guidance. Existing tools are built for Western travel or booking, not the experience of being somewhere new in India.
